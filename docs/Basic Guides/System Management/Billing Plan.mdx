Tier0 allows you to change your billing plan directly on the platform to suit different usage needs.

## Upgrading Billing Plans
1. Log in to Tier0, click your avatar at the upper-right corner, and then click **Settings**.
2. Click **Billing & Plan** under **Workspace**, and then click **Upgrade Plan**.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/56.png" />

3. Select a plan.
- **Pro**: Directly upgrade on the platform after payment.
- **Enterprise**: Contact our sales team and schedule a meeting to customize your plan.

<img width={350} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/57.png" />

:::info
Only the workspace owner can upgrade the billing plan.
:::

4. Complete the payment as prompted.

<img width={350} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/58.png" />

## Managing Billing Information and Invoices
1. Log in to Tier0, click your avatar at the upper-right corner, and then click **Settings**.
2. Click **Billing & Plan** under **Workspace**, and then click **Manage** next to **Edit Billing Information**.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/59.png" />

3. Enter your billing information and click **Save**.
4. Click **Manage** next to **Invoices & Payments** to view your invoices.

<img width={550} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/60.png" />

5. Click the invoice under **INVOICE HISTORY** to download it.

<img width={350} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/61.png" />

- Invoice: Contains invoice number, billing date, billing information, plan details, and amount paid.
- Receipt: Contains receipt number, payment date, plan details, amount paid and payment history.
