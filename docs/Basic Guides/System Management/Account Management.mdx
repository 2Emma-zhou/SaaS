You can set the basic profile and system preferences of your Tier0 account.

## Setting Account Profile
1. Log in to Tier0, click your avatar at the upper-right corner, and then click **Settings**.

<img width={350} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/38.png" />

2. Click **Profile**, and then modify your profile as needed, including avatar, nickname, and password and more.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/39.png" />

- Change avatar: Click the avatar to upload a new image.
- Change nickname: Click <img src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/40.png" /> next to **Nickname** and enter a new one.
- Click **Open** next to **Privacy Policy** to view the privacy policy of the platform.
- Change password: Click **Change Password**, and enter the verification code sent to your email, and then change the password.

<img width={350} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/41.png" />

- Log out: Click <img width={20} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/42.png" /> next to **Log out** to log out of the platform.
- Delete account: Click **Delete Account** to delete your account. After deletion, all your data will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered. Please operate with caution.

<img width={350} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/174-1.png" />

## Setting System Preferences

1. Log in to Tier0, click your avatar at the upper-right corner, and then click **Settings**.
2. Click **Preferences**, and then select the system language between **Chinese** and **English**.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/43.png" />
