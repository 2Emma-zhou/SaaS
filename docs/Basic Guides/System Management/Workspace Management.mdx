Data on the platform is managed independently by workspaces. Each workspace has its own UNS models, flows, data source connections, and other resources.

## Inviting Users to Workspaces
1. Log in to Tier0, click on your avatar and then click **Invite**.

<img width={350} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/47.png" />

2. Enter the email addresses of users to invite, set their roles, and then click **Send**.
- **Admin**: Can manage workspace settings and members.
- **Member**: Can use workspace.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/48.png" />

3. (Optional) Click **Generate Invitation Code**, and send it to users to join the workspace directly.

<img width={350} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/49.png" />

## Adding Workspace
1. Log in to Tier0, click on your avatar and then click **Add workspace**.
2. Either enter the invitation code to join a workspace or to apply for a new personal or enterprise workspace.

<img width={350} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/3.png" />

## Configuring Workspace

1. Log in to Tier0, click on your avatar, and then click **Settings**.
2. Under **Workspace**, click **General**, and then configure the workspace as needed.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/50.png" />

- Change workspace avatar/name/description: Click corresponding fields to change workspace information.
- Leave workspace: Click **Leave** next to **Leave Workspace** to leave the workspace permanently.
- Transfer ownership: Click **Transfer** next to **Transfer Ownership**, select a member to transfer workspace ownership to.
- Delete workspace: Click **Delete** next to **Delete Workspace** to delete the workspace permanently.

<img width={350} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/51.png" />

:::info
- Only admins and members can leave the workspace. Owners need to transfer ownership to other members before leaving.
- Only owners can transfer ownership/delete the workspace.
:::


## Managing Workspace Members
1. Log in to Tier0, click on your avatar, and then click **Settings**.
2. Under **Workspace**, click **Members**, and then manage workspace members, including invite new members, delete current members, change roles and more.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/52.png" />

:::info
Same roles cannot act on each other. For example, an admin cannot change the role of another admin, but can change the role of a member.
:::

## Managing Workspace Plans

Tier0 offers different billing plans for personal and enterprise users. You can view the details of each plan and upgrade your plan as needed.
:::info
Only the workspace owner can upgrade the plan.
:::

1. Log in to Tier0, click your avatar at the upper-right corner, and then click **Settings**.
2. Click **Plan** under **Workspace**, and then click **Upgrade Plan**.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/56.png" />

3. Select **Personal**, and view the details of each plan, click **Email Sales** and get in touch with our sales team.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/57.png" />

4. (optional) For business users, select **Business** to view the details of each plan, click **Email Sales** and proceed with our sales team.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/132.png" />

## Controlling Workspace Data Access

:::info
Authentication keys are for API access to the data in your workspace. Check out the [API documentation]((https://clouddoc.tier0.dev/openapi)) for more details.
:::
You can use API to get data in your workspace with authentication keys.
1. Log in to Tier0, click on your avatar, and then click **Settings**.
2. Under **Workspace**, click **API Keys**, and select a type of API key to create. 

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/134.png" />

- **Service Key**: Workspace owner and admin can create this key with self-defined permissions.
- **Personal Key**: Members can only create this key with **Read-only** permission.

3. Click **Create Service Key**, and enter the key name, set the permissions, and then click **Save**.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/135.png" />

4. Copy the generated key and use it in your API calls.

<img width={350} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/136.png" />

:::info
- Do the same to create a personal key if needed, but remember to keep the key secure and do not share it with others.
- You can edit/delete the keys by clicking <img width={20} src="http://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/119.png" /> next to each key.
:::
