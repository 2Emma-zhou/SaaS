Data on the platform is isolated by workspaces. Each workspace has its own UNS models, flows, data source connections, and users.

## Creating Workspaces
1. Log in to Tier0, click on your avatar and then click **Add workspace**.

<img width={350} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/45.png" />

2. Enter the workspace name and click **Save**.


## Joining Workspaces
1. Log in to Tier0, click on your avatar and then click **Add workspace**.
2. Enter the invitation code of the workspace and click **Save**.

<img width={350} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/46.png" />


## Inviting Users to Workspaces
1. Log in to Tier0, click on your avatar and then click **Invite**.

<img width={350} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/47.png" />

2. Enter the email addresses of users to invite, set their roles, and then click **Send**.
- **Admin**: Can manage workspace settings and members.
- **Member**: Can use workspace.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/48.png" />

3. (Optional) Click **Generate Invitation Code**, and send it to users to join the workspace directly.

<img width={350} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/49.png" />

## Configuring Workspace

1. Log in to Tier0, click on your avatar, and then click **Settings**.
2. Under **Workspace**, click **General**, and then configure the workspace as needed.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/50.png" />

- Change workspace avatar/name/description: Click corresponding fields to change workspace information.
- Leave workspace: Click **Leave** next to **Leave Workspace** to leave the workspace permanently.
:::info
Only members can leave the workspace. Admins need to transfer ownership to other members before leaving.
:::
- Transfer ownership: Click **Transfer** next to **Transfer Ownership**, select a member to transfer workspace ownership to.
:::info
Only admins can transfer ownership.
:::
- Delete workspace: Click **Delete** next to **Delete Workspace** to delete the workspace permanently.

<img width={350} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/51.png" />

## Managing Workspace Members
1. Log in to Tier0, click on your avatar, and then click **Settings**.
2. Under **Workspace**, click **Members**, and then manage workspace members, including invite new members, delete current members, change roles and more.
