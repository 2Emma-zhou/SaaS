Tier0 adopts AI agent inside the UNS module, enabling you to interact with the UNS models simply by talking to the agent in natural language.

## How to Talk to UNS Models
1. Log in to Tier0, and then select **UNS**.
2. Select the **Canvas** tab on the top, so that later you can see the cards generated by the agent based on your instruction.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/105.png" />

3. In the chat window on the right, drag a topic from the left side and drop it into the chat window, then enter your instruction to talk to the agent.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/106.png" />

4. Drag the generated cards to the canvas, and you can see the visualized data.
:::tip
- Click <img width={20} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/107.png" /> at the lower-left corner of the conversation message to send the corresponding instruction again.
- Click **Card List** at the top to see all the generated cards in the current session.
- Click <img width={20} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/108.png" /> at the upper-right corner to start a new session.
:::
5. (optional) Click <img width={20} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/110.png" /> at the upper-right corner of the card to add it to the chat or delete it.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/109.png" />
