The digital twin feature is available on the platform to help you visually build data models.

## Uploading Digital Twin Models
1. Log in to Tier0, select **Anchor** > **Model Library**.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/62.png" />

2. Click <img src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/63.png" />, select a **.glb** model, and upload it to the platform.

## Mapping UNS Data to Digital Twin Models
:::info
There are 2 types of data mapping with 3D models.
- Static Mapping: Displays UNS data as a static tag on the scene. Often used in Gussian Models (no movable parts but in need of data display) or static attributes of structured models (eg., temperature of AC).
- Motion Mapping: Data mapped with the movable parts of the model, and the parts  move according to the data change.
::: 
1. After uploading the model, click the model to start mapping it with UNS data.
2. Select model nodes to be mapped, and click **Confirm**.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/67.png" />

3. Click **Create Instance**, select UNS data model that controls the model animation.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/68.png" />

4. Enter the **Instance Name**, set the **Real-world Height** of the model, and click **Create**.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/81.png" />

### Motion Mapping
Bind the UNS data with movable parts of the model.
1. On the **Model Mapping** tab, click <img width={25} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/82.png" />, and select the position axis of the model to be controlled.
    
    <img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/83.png" />

2. Map the data field with the selected axis.

    <img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/84.png" />

### Static Mapping
Bind the UNS data with the model as a tag.
1. Click **Data Tagging**, and click **Add Tag**.

    <img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/85.png" />

2. Click on the model to select the position of the tag, and configure the tag attributes.

    <img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/86.png" />

3. Set the motion tags visibility mode, and click **Preview**.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/87.png" />


## Viewing Digital Twin Models
1. After mapping UNS data to the model, click **Generate QR Code** at the upper-right corner.
2. Select **Share Model**, and click **Generate**.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/88.png" />

3. Scan the QR code to view the digital twin model on your mobile device.

<img width={350} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/89.png" />

:::info
You can also download the code, or copy the code link for later use.
:::

## Quick Operations during Mapping
:::info
Node mappings are saved automatically.
:::
#### On the Model Window

- Drag the slider of **Explosion** at the bottom of the model window to view the model parts.
- Click <img width={25} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/68-1.png" /> to display the model in live with mapped UNS data.
- Click <img width={25} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/68-2.png" /> to dispaly the model in original state.
- Click <img width={25} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/68-3.png" /> to view the model in full screen.

#### On the Mapping Tab

- Click **Edit Selection** at the upper-right corner to modify selected topics.
- Click <img src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/70.png" /> to disconnect a model node from the data.
- Click <img src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/6.png" /> to add payload to UNS topics for quick mapping with new data.

<img width={350} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/71.png" />
