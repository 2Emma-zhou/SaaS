The digital twin feature is available on the platform to help you visually build data models.

## Uploading Digital Twin Models
1. Log in to Tier0, select **App** > **Anchor**.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/62.png" />

:::tip
Click **Load Sample** to view step-by-step instructions using a sample model.
:::

2. Click <img src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/63.png" />, and select a **.glb** model to upload.
3. Click **Open** to upload the model to the platform.

## Mapping UNS Data to Digital Twin Models
1. After uploading the model, click the model.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/64.png" />

2. Click <img src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/65.png" /> under **Real-world height**, and set the real height of the model.
3. Click <img src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/66.png" /> under **Topic** to select or create UNS topics for binding to model nodes.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/67.png" />

4. Click **Save**, select nodes that control the model animation.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/68.png" />

:::tip
- Drag the slider of **Explosion** at the model bottom to view the model parts.
- Click <img width={25} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/68-1.png" /> to display the model in live with mapped UNS data.
- Click <img width={25} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/68-2.png" /> to dispaly the model in original state.
- Click <img width={25} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/68-3.png" /> to view model in full screen.
:::
5. Click **Confirm**, and then start mapping UNS data to the model for animation control.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/69.png" />

:::info
- Node mappings are saved automatically.
- Click **Edit Selection** at the upper-right corner to modify selected nodes.
- Click <img src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/70.png" /> to disconnect a model node from the data.
- Click <img src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/6.png" /> to add payload to UNS topics for quick mapping with new data.

<img width={350} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/71.png" />

:::

## Viewing Digital Twin Models
1. After mapping UNS data to the model, click **View QR Code** at the upper-right corner.
2. Scan the QR code to view the digital twin model on your mobile device.
