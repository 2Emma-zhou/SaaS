# Access Control

Tier0 adopts Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). Roles are divided into 3 dimensions: Platform roles, project roles, and business roles.

## Platform Roles

Control access to the entire workspace.

<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Role</th>
<th>Permissions</th>
<th>Description</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>Owner</td>
<td>Deletes/transfers the workspace, manages admins and members, basic modifications on the workspace, full access to all features.</td>
<td>Owns the workspace, highest level of access.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Admin</td>
<td>Manages members, basic modifications on the workspace, full access to all features.</td>
<td>Has administrative privileges within the workspace. Cannot delete or transfer the workspace.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Member</td>
<td>Sole access to <b>Launchpad</b>, can leave the workspace.</td>
<td>Has limited access to the workspace.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Custom Role</td>
<td>Access to set features, can leave the workspace.</td>
<td>Custom access control.</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

## Project Roles

Control access to projects.

<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Role</th>
<th>Permissions</th>
<th>Description</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>Creator</td>
<td>Creates and manages projects, assigns roles to members.</td>
<td>Owns the project, has the highest level of access.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Editor</td>
<td>Manages the project, assigns roles to members.</td>
<td>Has editing permissions within the project. Cannot delete the project.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Operator</td>
<td>Uses the project.</td>
<td>Use of the project, cannot edit anything.</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

## Business Roles

Control access to specific business applications within a project.

Business roles are assigned by the project **Creator** or **Editor** to project **Operators**.

:::info
Business roles are configured during application buidling.
:::
