Tier0 provides an audit log to track all operations performed by users under the current workspace.
:::info
Only available for workspaces on the **Scale** plan.
:::

## Viewing Audit Logs
1. Log in to Tier0, click your avatar at the upper-right corner, and then click **Settings**.
2. Click **Audit Logs** under **Workspace**.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/125.png" />

- Click **Filter**, and set the filter conditions based on **Module** or **Action** to view specific logs.

    <img width={350} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/126.png" />

- Set the start and end date to view logs within a specific time range.

    <img width={350} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/127.png" />
